# No-boundary experiment

The canonical EXNIHILO experiment treats the generator output as one continuous stream of independent random characters.

There are:

- no generated words;
- no generated sentences;
- no spaces required by the generator;
- no vocabulary;
- no dictionary during generation;
- no corpus;
- no grammar;
- no language model;
- no detector feedback.

The analyzer may segment the completed stream afterward for measurement. Any segmentation is an **analysis operation**, not a generation rule.

This distinction prevents the experiment from quietly manufacturing word boundaries before asking whether recognizable language appears.
