# EXNIHILO prior-art note

EXNIHILO does **not** claim to be the first random-text experiment.

Known precedents include Claude Shannon's zero-order random character experiments and later work on random-letter/random-word generation and the infinite-monkey idea.

The EXNIHILO research question is narrower:

> What recognizable English structure occurs in a genuinely unconstrained random character stream when the generator receives no vocabulary, dictionary, corpus, grammar, language model, or semantic guidance?

The generator and detector are deliberately separated. English resources may be used by the detector to classify already-generated output, but never to steer generation.

Novelty, if any, must be established by a fuller literature/code search before making a "first" claim.
